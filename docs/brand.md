# Paytm Saathi — Brand Guidelines

> **The autonomous operations teammate for every merchant.**

**Product name:** Paytm Saathi  
**Category:** Autonomous AI Merchant Operations Assistant  
**Working tagline:** From payment problems to verified resolutions.  
**Brand personality:** Trustworthy, helpful, proactive, capable, transparent

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand Idea

Paytm Saathi is an AI operations teammate that helps merchants resolve everyday payment and device issues. It understands the merchant's situation, investigates relevant system data, takes permitted actions, verifies the result, and brings in a human operations teammate when a case needs human judgment or approval.

It is not positioned as a generic chatbot. It is positioned as an action-oriented operations partner.

### Mission

Help merchants get back to business quickly by making payment and device support more proactive, dependable, and easy to understand.

### Vision

A world where every merchant has a reliable digital operations teammate working alongside them.

### Brand Promise

**We investigate, act safely, verify, and keep the merchant informed.**

### Core Principles

1. **Action over conversation** — Move beyond suggestions to permitted, useful actions.
2. **Merchant-first** — Respect the urgency and realities of running a shop.
3. **Trust through transparency** — Explain what was checked, what was done, and what remains unresolved.
4. **Safety by design** — Enforce permissions and business rules outside the language model.
5. **Human partnership** — Escalate when human judgment, approval, or physical intervention is needed.
6. **Verified outcomes** — Never claim an issue is fixed until the available system evidence confirms it.

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Small and medium-sized merchants in India.
- Kirana stores and local retailers.
- Merchants using QR payments and Soundbox devices.
- Merchants who need quick, clear help during business hours.

### Secondary Audience

- Merchant support and operations teams.
- Field-service coordinators and technicians.
- Operations managers monitoring service quality.

### Audience Needs

Merchants need to know whether a payment was successful, why a device is not working, what can be done immediately, and whether a person needs to follow up.

Operations teams need a concise case history, reliable diagnostics, safe action controls, and a clear handoff.

## 3. Brand Positioning and Messaging

### Positioning Statement

For merchants who depend on digital payments throughout the day, Paytm Saathi is an autonomous merchant operations teammate that investigates payment and device issues, performs authorized recovery actions, and coordinates human support when needed.

Unlike a conventional support chatbot, Paytm Saathi is designed to work across operational tools and verify the outcome of each action.

### Short Description

Paytm Saathi helps merchants resolve payment and Soundbox issues through contextual investigation, safe actions, and verified outcomes — with human support when required.

### Elevator Pitch

A payment issue can interrupt a merchant's business in seconds. Paytm Saathi works like a digital operations teammate: it checks the merchant's context, investigates transactions and device health, takes permitted corrective actions, and verifies whether the issue is resolved.

If the case needs human judgment or on-site support, it hands over the investigation to the operations team.

### Key Messages

| Message | What It Means |
|---|---|
| More than answers | The agent can take permitted actions through connected tools. |
| Context-aware support | It considers merchant, transaction, and device information. |
| Safe autonomy | Deterministic policies control sensitive actions. |
| Human when it matters | Complex or approval-required cases are escalated with context. |
| Outcomes you can inspect | Actions and results are recorded in an audit timeline. |

### Recommended Product Language

**Use:**
- AI operations teammate
- Merchant operations assistant
- Investigate and resolve
- Authorized action
- Verified resolution
- Human handoff
- Operations team
- Device diagnostics
- Transaction verification

**Avoid:**
- Fully replaces human support
- Guarantees every issue is resolved
- Instant refunds or guaranteed credits
- Connected to Paytm's live internal systems (unless that integration is genuinely available)
- 100% autonomous
- Zero downtime

## 4. Brand Personality and Voice

### Personality

**Helpful, not chatty.** Be warm and direct, especially when the merchant is under pressure.

**Capable, not overconfident.** State what the system knows and distinguish verified facts from likely causes.

**Proactive, not intrusive.** Surface useful warnings and next steps without overwhelming the merchant.

**Transparent, not technical.** Explain outcomes in everyday language. Keep technical diagnostics available to operations staff.

### Voice Principles

1. Lead with the merchant's immediate concern.
2. Use short sentences and familiar words.
3. Confirm important facts before taking action.
4. Explain when an action is being attempted, completed, or awaiting approval.
5. Never invent a transaction status, device state, or successful outcome.
6. When uncertain, say so and offer the next safe step.

### Example Merchant Messages

**Issue received**

"Samajh gaya. Main pehle ₹250 payment ka status aur aapke Soundbox ki condition check karta hoon."

**Investigation result**

"Payment successful dikh raha hai. Soundbox ka volume bahut low set hai."

**Action in progress**

"Main volume level badhane ki koshish kar raha hoon. Ek moment dijiye."

**Verified result**

"Device ne naya volume level confirm kar diya hai. Ab main announcement replay kar raha hoon."

**Escalation**

"Device mein hardware error mila hai. Isse remotely fix nahi kiya ja sakta. Maine aapka case support team ko diagnostics ke saath bhej diya hai."

**Uncertain result**

"Command bhej di gayi hai, lekin device se confirmation nahi mila. Main ise resolved mark nahi karunga; support team ko case bhej raha hoon."

These are sample interface messages. Use the language selected by the merchant and keep critical amounts, statuses, and references unambiguous.

## 5. Visual Identity

### Visual Direction

The visual identity should feel dependable, modern, and operational. It should sit comfortably alongside a familiar payments brand without copying or implying official Paytm brand assets that have not been provided.

**Design Keywords:** Clear, confident, friendly, precise, calm, efficient.

### Typography

Recommended:

- **Inter** for product UI and dashboards.
- Use a clear hierarchy with medium-weight headings and highly readable body text.
- Use tabular numerals for amounts, transaction IDs, percentages, and timestamps.

### Logo Direction

Create a simple wordmark reading **Paytm Saathi** with a distinct companion/connection symbol.

Possible symbol concepts:

- Two connected forms representing merchant and AI teammate.
- A subtle speech mark combined with a check to suggest communication and verified action.
- A simple linked-node mark suggesting coordinated operations.

Keep the mark legible at small sizes. Do not recreate the Paytm logo or use official Paytm marks without authorization.

*Note: Brand colors, color palette, and design tokens will be maintained in a separate document.*

## 6. Product Experience Principles

### Merchant Experience

- Make the main action obvious: describe the issue by text or voice.
- Support natural, concise Hindi/Hinglish and English interactions where implemented.
- Show progress during investigation and execution.
- Display transaction references and amounts clearly.
- Explain whether the issue is resolved, still being checked, or escalated.
- Avoid exposing internal technical details unless useful to the merchant.

### Operations Cockpit

- Prioritize cases by urgency and merchant impact.
- Separate autonomous actions from actions awaiting approval.
- Show the evidence behind the agent's recommendation.
- Provide a chronological tool and decision timeline.
- Make the human's next action clear.
- Record approvals, execution results, and failures.

### Status Vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Investigating | The agent is collecting and checking information. |
| Action in progress | An authorized action is being attempted. |
| Verification pending | The action was attempted; the result is not yet confirmed. |
| Resolved | Available system evidence confirms the intended outcome. |
| Needs approval | A human must approve the proposed action. |
| Escalated | The case has been handed to a human team. |
| Unable to resolve | The available recovery steps did not solve the issue. |

## 7. Brand Storytelling

### The Problem

For a merchant, a silent Soundbox or uncertain payment is not a minor technical inconvenience. It can interrupt a sale, create confusion at the counter, and consume valuable time.

### The Response

Paytm Saathi brings together merchant context, transaction checks, device diagnostics, authorized actions, and human operations support in one coordinated workflow.

### The Outcome

The merchant gets a clear answer and, where possible, a verified fix. The operations team receives the context and evidence needed to handle cases that require human intervention.

### Core Story

**A teammate that doesn't stop at "Here's what you can try." It works through the problem, takes the next safe action, and checks what happened.**

## 8. Demo and Communication Guidelines

For hackathon presentations and prototypes:

- Clearly identify simulated Paytm services and sample data as mock integrations.
- Do not imply access to production Paytm, NPCI, bank, or Soundbox infrastructure unless it exists.
- Distinguish a successful API or simulator acknowledgement from proof of a physical device outcome.
- Do not present target automation rates, cost savings, or resolution times as measured results.
- Show the agent's investigation, policy checks, tool calls, action results, and verification.
- Demonstrate at least one successful autonomous resolution and one appropriate human escalation.

### Suggested Demo Headline

**A merchant has a payment problem. Paytm Saathi doesn't just explain what to do — it investigates, acts, verifies, and brings in a human when needed.**

## 9. Brand Boilerplate

### One-line Description

Paytm Saathi is an autonomous AI operations teammate that helps merchants investigate and resolve payment and device issues.

### Short Description

Paytm Saathi works alongside merchants and operations teams to diagnose issues, take authorized actions, verify outcomes, and escalate complex cases with context.

### Extended Description

Paytm Saathi is designed to make merchant support more action-oriented. It uses merchant context, transaction information, and device diagnostics to investigate operational issues.

Through connected tools and deterministic safety policies, it can perform permitted recovery actions and verify their results. When a case requires human judgment, approval, or physical intervention, it prepares a structured handoff for the operations team.

---

## 10. Brand Guardrails

1. Paytm Saathi is a proposed hackathon product concept; do not imply official Paytm endorsement or production deployment.
2. Use Paytm names and marks only in accordance with applicable brand permissions.
3. Never claim an action succeeded without a corresponding result or verification.
4. Never imply that the AI can authorize unrestricted financial adjustments.
5. Keep sensitive merchant and customer information out of public demos and screenshots.
6. Make mock data and simulated integrations clear to judges and viewers.
7. Preserve a clear human approval path for sensitive, high-value, disputed, or physical-service actions.

## Brand Essence

**A dependable teammate that turns merchant problems into safely executed, verified outcomes.**
